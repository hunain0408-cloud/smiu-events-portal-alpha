Tech Innovation Summit

A full day of talks, demos and networking on emerging technologies.

About the Event

The Tech Innovation Summit brings together students, faculty and industry experts to explore artificial intelligence, cloud computing and cyber security. Attend keynote talks, see live product demos and connect with companies looking for interns.

Event Information

  • Date: 05 Dec 2026
  • Time: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Venue: Main Auditorium, SMIU
  • Fee: Free for SMIU students

Event Guide

  • 10:00 AM - Opening ceremony and welcome note
  • 11:00 AM - Keynote: The Future of AI in Pakistan
  • 1:00 PM - Lunch break and networking
  • 2:00 PM - Live product demos by student teams
  • 3:30 PM - Panel discussion and closing

  • Software engineering lead from a Karachi technology company
  • Cyber security researcher and SMIU alumnus
  • Faculty members of the Department of Computer Science

  • Bring your university ID card for entry.
  • Registration closes two days before the event.
  • Seats are limited and given on a first-come, first-served basis.
